Printing & Apparel Story

The PXL Prints Journey

From a single manual press to a full-scale custom apparel studio, our journey has been stitched together by craft, creativity, and the brands who trust us with their designs.

01

From Humble Beginnings

PXL Prints started in 2014 in a small garage with one manual screen press and a big idea: make custom apparel that people are proud to wear. Every shirt was printed and inspected by hand.

02

Milestones & Achievements

Within a few years we outgrew the garage, opened our first production facility, and printed our 100,000th garment. Awards for print quality and turnaround followed close behind.

03

Innovation & Growth

We invested in direct-to-garment, DTF, and automatic screen printing technology so brands could order anything from a single sample to ten thousand units with vivid, durable color every time.

04

Our Global Reach

Today PXL Prints ships custom-printed apparel to startups, sports teams, festivals, and Fortune 500 brands across the world, with fulfillment partners on three continents.

05

Looking Ahead

As we look to the future, we are committed to water-based eco inks, organic blanks, and on-demand printing that reduces waste, so your brand looks good and does good.
