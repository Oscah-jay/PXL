Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our printing services, orders, and more

We offer multiple printing methods including screen printing, embroidery, digital printing, heat transfer, applique, and laser etching. Each method is suited for different materials and design requirements. Our team can recommend the best option for your specific project.

For most products, we require a minimum order of 10 units. However, for bulk corporate orders, we can accommodate larger quantities with special pricing. Contact our team to discuss your specific needs and minimum quantity requirements.

Standard delivery typically takes 7-10 business days after order confirmation. We also offer expedited shipping for rush orders (3-5 business days). Delivery times may vary based on order complexity and destination. Free shipping is available on orders over $148.99.

Absolutely! Our design team can assist you with creating or refining your artwork. We offer design consultation services and can provide mockups before production. You can also upload your own designs, and we'll ensure they meet our printing specifications.

We welcome custom designs and special requests! Whether you need unique color combinations, special materials, or specific sizing, our team will work with you to bring your vision to life. We specialize in custom orders for corporate clients and startups.
