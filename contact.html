Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order is just 1 item! However, bulk orders (10+ units) qualify for discounted pricing. Contact us for volume quotes.

How long does printing take?

Standard turnaround is 3-5 business days. Rush orders available. Timeline depends on quantity and complexity of design.

Can you help with design?

Absolutely! Our design team can help refine your concepts. Provide your ideas and we'll create mockups for your approval.

What printing methods do you offer?

We specialize in screen printing, direct-to-garment (DTG), and sublimation printing. Perfect for logos, designs, and full-color graphics.

Do you offer international shipping?

We currently ship within the United States. Contact us about international orders and shipping options.
